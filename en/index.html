For anyone who has loved and lost a pet

Your love for them doesn't end here.

PetLoss is a private, on-device companion for pet-loss grief — gently hold your memories, say the unsaid, and heal at your own pace. No accounts. No uploads. No pressure to move on.

“The house is quieter now. Some days the weight comes without warning — at 3 a.m., at the food bowl, in the doorway they used to wait in.”

We built PetLoss for those moments. Not to rush your grief — but so you never have to carry it alone.

Four moments when you need to be held

Grief isn't linear. PetLoss meets you where you are — gently, privately, on your device.

🌊

When grief suddenly overwhelms

One tap of SOS brings grounding words, gentle guidance, and immediate comfort in the heaviest moments.

🕊️

When you need to say the unsaid

Write to your pet anytime. Guided journaling, story ideas, and short expressions all feel valid — no pressure to write a lot.

✨

When you want to keep them close

Turn favorite photos into cards, posters, cinematic tribute videos, printable plaques — and now, a living wallpaper.

🕯️

When hard dates return

Memory timeline, daily rituals, and gentle reminders help you honor them on anniversaries and difficult days.

New · Spatial Wallpaper

They can stay, softly, on your home screen

Choose a portrait photo from your Photos and PetLoss turns it into a spatial wallpaper — a living, breathing presence with gentle depth, softly drifting on your lock screen and home screen.

No setup, no editing, no saving steps. Open the app, pick a photo, and let their warmth stay close.
